
# Embodied AI (by NVIDIA)
Source: https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/embodied-ai/
Integration of trained AI model inside of a robot with the aim to allow autonomous operations.

## Building Embodied AI
### Pretraining - AI
Teach AI fundamental skills and knowledge before fine tuned for specific task. Done by using Web, Real-World or Synthetic data.

### Post-Training - Synthetic data and simulation
Allows the fine tuning of the the model for specific tasks. Can be done trough:
- Synthetic Data in Simulation - training the model on variety of scenarios and edge cases, enhancing robustness and performance
- Reinforcement learning - training the model trough trial and error, rewarding or penalizing actions, allows the robot to adapts to new situations before being released in the real world
- Imitation learning - repeating actions defined by human experts. Allows the AI to progress faster towards the wanted result.
### Inferencing and Runtime technology
Real time application connecting the AI with the real world
- Computer vision - allows the AI to interpret its surroundings. Used for navigation, scene understanding, allowing AI to perceive surroundings. 
- Large Language Models (LLMs) - the connection to the human communication. Allows understanding and responding to human speech, as well as conveying complex information.
- Vision Language Model (VLMs) - building on top of the LLMs, enhancing cognitive and and interactive capabilities.

### Embodied AI Use cases

Smart Spaces - AMRs navigating trough dynamic warehouses, transporting resources to locations, etc. Using computer vision to understand the surroundings.

Humanoids and others - Performing tasks that could be harmful to a human or play supportive role in human operational tasks. They can also take on repetitive  tasks where the human is not in full potential/need
