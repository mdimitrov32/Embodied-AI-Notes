# Research areas defined in the Project Proposal doc
- Agentic AI integration requires research to bridge GenAI/LLMs (as cognitive engines) to physical cyber-physical systems via the Omniverse, including world models for robust behavior.
  
- Behavior trees enriched with Omnigraph require investigation of decision logic, conditions, actions, fallbacks, and real-time replanning in ROS2 contexts.
  
- Demonstrator scenarios (docking, handovers) involve research on robot physics simulation in ISAAC SIM and GenAI-driven autonomy, problem-solving, and error correction.
  
- Analysis of software development benefits for the Fontys team requires an evaluative study of how world models improve robot robustness compared to traditional programming.
